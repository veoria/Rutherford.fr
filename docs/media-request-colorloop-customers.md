# Media request to ColorLoop customers

Email in English asking printers running ColorLoop for photos and video of their
installation, to use on the blog, the site and social.

Two pieces: the email itself, short enough to be read on a phone, and a one page
brief they can forward to whoever actually holds the camera. Send the brief as an
attachment or paste it below the signature, do not make the email long.

---

# 2. The one page brief

**Filming and photographing your ColorLoop installation**

Fifteen minutes, a phone, no production needed. Shoot **landscape**, hold still
for three seconds at the start and end of every clip, and keep the phone away
from moving parts.

## The shots that are always useful

**The press itself**
Wide shot of the whole line, and a closer one on the units. If the brand name is
visible on the frame, include it. Printers recognise their own machine before
they read a word.

**The console**
The screen showing ColorLoop running on a live job, the ink zone bars, the
measurement device on the bar. This is the shot we use most and the one we
almost never have enough of.

**Someone working**
An operator at the console, reading a sheet under the viewing booth, marking a
plate, checking a stack. A person in the frame does more for a photo than any
amount of clean machinery.

**The printed result**
A sheet or a finished job, ideally something with a strong flat brand color.
Please tell us if a job is under NDA and we will leave it out.

**Optional but excellent**
Ten to twenty seconds of the press actually running, with the sound.

## What makes a shot unusable

▪ Vertical video, if it is destined for the site rather than social
▪ Very dark or backlit frames, the pressroom lighting is usually enough
▪ Heavy filters or beauty modes
▪ Screenshots of a screen photographed at an angle, stand square to it

## Practical

▪ **Format:** send the originals, not compressed copies. WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive is fine.
▪ **Faces:** if a colleague is recognisable, please check they are happy to appear. We will crop or blur anyone who would rather not.
▪ **Confidentiality:** tell us which customer work must not be identifiable and we will keep it out.
▪ **Credit:** tell us exactly how you want your company named, and give us the URL you want us to link to.

Send everything to {{owner_email}}, or reply to this email and we will open a
transfer link for you.

# Notes for us

**The backlink argument is the real one, do not overstate it.** What we offer is
an editorial mention with a followed link from an industry domain. That is
genuinely worth having and worth saying plainly. It is not worth dressing up.

**Ask for the naming and the URL up front.** Getting the photos and then chasing
the legal entity name and the right link for two weeks is what actually slows
these down.

**The NDA question has to be in the brief.** Packaging printers work under
customer confidentiality, and a beautiful shot of a carton we cannot publish
wastes their time and ours.

**Where these end up.** Blog articles, the case studies section of the homepage,
LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube. Photos of a real installation are also what stops
us reaching for stock or generated images, which our audience spots immediately.
