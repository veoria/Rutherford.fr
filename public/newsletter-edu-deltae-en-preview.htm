What to remember

One number — but not just any number

DeltaE measures the gap between the printed color and the target. DeltaE00 (the most used today) tracks how the eye actually perceives it. The question is which threshold to aim for, depending on what you print.

Process colors  — a tolerance around ΔE00 2 to 3 is realistic in production.
Brand colors  — demand tighter: that's where the client's eye judges.
Holding it over time  — a tolerance only counts if it's measured every shift, not once a month.
