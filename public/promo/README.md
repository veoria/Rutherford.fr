# Promo — account tours (presentation animations)

Standalone reels presenting the Rutherford customer space through the eyes of a
**fictional account**. Two account types are covered:

**Reseller account** (green accent):
- **`reseller-account.html`** — calm "screencast" feel (cross-fades, simulated cursor).
- **`reseller-account-motion.html`** — **motion-design** version: boxes arrive one by one
  (or grouped when coherent) with a spring and fly out between scenes (zoom-with-blur,
  off-page slides with rotation, shrink-to-zero).

**X-Rite distributor account** (orange accent, X-Rite PANTONE co-brand):
- **`xrite-distributor-account.html`** — motion-design version. Same engine, but the
  distributor oversees a **reseller network** (My network tab) instead of direct clients.

They present three sections of the Rutherford customer space:

1. **My account** — the reseller hub "Atlas Graphic Solutions" and its 5 clients
   (Imprimerie Berton, Cartonnages Vasseur, Helvetica Print AG, Grafica Lombarda,
   Nordpack Druck); the distributor reel ("Spectra Color Distribution") instead shows its
   network of resellers (Atlas Graphic, ColorPoint Benelux, PrintCare Iberia, GrafSysteme DACH).
2. **Console Validation** — press-validation tracking, with one request moving from
   "In review" to "Connectable".
3. **Support** — an in-progress support ticket, with activity timeline and conversation.

> ⚠️ Simulation. Every company and person mentioned is **fictional** and is not a real
> Rutherford customer.

## How to watch it

- **Simplest:** open either `.html` file directly in a browser (double-click). The
  files are 100% standalone — no dependencies, no server, work offline.
- It auto-plays on a loop; the dots at the bottom jump to a scene, and "Replay" restarts it.
- **To turn it into a video** (LinkedIn, sales deck…): open the file full-screen and use a
  screen recorder (QuickTime on Mac, the Xbox Game Bar on Windows, or OBS). Suggested
  format: 16:9.

## Hosted (hidden) pages on the site

The motion-design reels are also served as **hidden pages** (`noindex, nofollow`, not
linked from any menu — reachable only with the URL):

- **`/promo`** (`rutherford.fr/promo`) — reseller account. Rendered by `app/promo/page.tsx`,
  module `app/promo/animation.ts` (from `reseller-account-motion.html`).
- **`/promo-xrite`** (`rutherford.fr/promo-xrite`) — X-Rite distributor account. Rendered by
  `app/promo-xrite/page.tsx`, module `app/promo-xrite/animation.ts`
  (from `xrite-distributor-account.html`).

Each page renders its reel inside an isolated `<iframe srcDoc>`. The `animation.ts` modules
are auto-generated by copying the matching standalone file's content into them.

The static `.html` files in this folder are **not** servable as-is on the deployed site
because `next.config.js` has a global `*.html → /` redirect — that is why the hosted
version goes through the `/promo` route instead. The `.html` files remain here purely for
local opening and screen-recording.
